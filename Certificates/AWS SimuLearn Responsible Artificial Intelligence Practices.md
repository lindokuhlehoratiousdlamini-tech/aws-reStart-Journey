# 📘Responsible Artificial Intelligence Practices
Awarded on 11 December 2025

**Overview:**

In this course, I learned about responsible AI practices and why they are important when building and deploying AI systems. I was introduced to what responsible AI is, how it is defined, and the key challenges it aims to address, such as fairness, bias, transparency, and accountability. I explored the core dimensions of responsible AI and how they guide ethical AI development.
